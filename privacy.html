Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Coachjames.channel (the "Service"), operated by Coach James ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects information when you connect a TikTok account to the Service.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information provided through TikTok Login Kit with your permission, such as your open ID, display name, and avatar.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok so the Service can upload and publish videos on your behalf.
  • Content you choose to post, such as video files, captions, and privacy settings.

We do not collect your TikTok password.

2. How we use information

  • To upload and publish videos to your TikTok account when you request it.
  • To display which account is connected and check the status of your posts.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Sharing

Your content and the necessary request data are sent to TikTok through its official APIs to complete the posts you request. We do not share your information with other third parties except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and related data are stored securely and are used only by the Service. We keep this information only as long as your TikTok account remains connected or as needed to provide the Service.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can revoke the Service's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold about you by emailing us. We will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
